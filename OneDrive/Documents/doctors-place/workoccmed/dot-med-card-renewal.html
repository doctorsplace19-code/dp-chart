Certificate Validity

How Long Is a DOT Medical Card Valid?

A DOT medical certificate (also called a Medical Examiner's Certificate, or MEC) is valid for up to 24 months from the date of examination. However, the examining physician may issue a shorter certificate if you have certain health conditions that require more frequent monitoring.

24 months
Full certificate
Blood pressure below 140/90 and no disqualifying conditions
12 months
Elevated BP or monitoring required
BP 140–159/90–99 or conditions requiring annual review
3 months
High BP or insulin use
BP 160–179/100–109 or new insulin-treated diabetes
ⓘ

Shorter certificates are common

Many drivers receive a 12-month certificate rather than 24 months. If your blood pressure, vision, or another condition is borderline, the examiner may choose a shorter period. Always check the expiration date on your MEC — don't assume it's 2 years.

Timing

When Should You Renew?

Renew your DOT medical card before it expires — ideally 2 to 4 weeks before the expiration date. You can complete your renewal exam at any time while your current certificate is still valid.

⚠

Renewing early does NOT extend your current card

If you renew 30 days early, your new certificate starts from the date of the exam — not from your old card's expiration date. You do not lose any days. Renewing early is always the safe move.

What Happens If Your Card Expires?

If your DOT medical certificate expires, your CDL is automatically downgraded by your state DMV to a non-CDL license. You cannot legally operate a commercial motor vehicle in interstate commerce until you pass a new DOT physical and your state updates your record.

Your CDL itself does not expire — but without a current medical certificate on file with the FMCSA National Registry and your state DMV, the CDL has no operating authority. Getting reinstated requires passing a new DOT physical and waiting for the state to process the update, which can take 1–5 business days.

✓

Can I drive while waiting for my new card?

No. Once expired, you cannot legally drive a CMV in interstate commerce — even if you just completed your renewal physical and are waiting for your state DMV to update the record. Keep your old card visible while it is still valid to avoid any gap.

Step-by-Step

How to Renew Your DOT Medical Card

The renewal process is identical to your original DOT physical — there is no separate "renewal" form. You simply complete a new FMCSA medical examination with a certified medical examiner.

1

Order online or walk in

Order your renewal DOT physical at portal.dot-physical.net. You'll receive authorization within 1 business hour. Or walk in to any of 15,000+ certified sites — no order required at all walk-in locations.

2

Complete the FMCSA physical exam

Bring a government-issued photo ID, your glasses or contacts if you use them, your hearing aids if applicable, and a list of any current medications. The exam takes 30–45 minutes.

3

Receive your new Medical Examiner's Certificate

If you pass, the examiner issues your new MEC on the spot. Keep the paper copy — you must carry it while driving a CMV.

4

FMCSA National Registry is updated automatically

The examining physician reports your results to the FMCSA National Registry within 24 hours. Your state DMV typically updates your record within 1–5 business days.

Exam Preparation

What to Bring to Your Renewal Exam

There is no special preparation required for a renewal DOT physical. Bring the same items you would for any DOT physical:

  • ✓ Government-issued photo ID (driver's license, passport, etc.)
  • ✓ Eyeglasses or contact lenses if you use them for driving
  • ✓ Hearing aids if you rely on them
  • ✓ Current list of medications (prescription and over-the-counter)
  • ✓ Any specialist clearance letters for conditions like sleep apnea, diabetes, or heart disease
  • ✓ Your current DOT medical certificate (helpful but not required)

If you have a condition that previously required a specialist clearance, bring updated documentation — the examiner will need to review it before certifying you.
