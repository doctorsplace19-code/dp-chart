What we coordinate

The full pre-placement checklist — handled.

Everything a hospital, skilled nursing facility, or clinical program requires before staff and students start on the floor.

💉

TB screening

Two-step TB skin test (PPD) or IGRA blood test, with chest X-ray follow-up when needed.

🧬

Immunization titers

MMR, varicella, and hepatitis B titers to document immunity — plus boosters where non-immune.

💊

Vaccines

Tdap, influenza, hepatitis B, and other required vaccinations for clinical staff.

⚕

Physicals & fit-for-duty

Pre-placement exams confirming staff can safely perform essential clinical functions.

🗡

Respirator clearance

OSHA-compliant N95 fit testing and respirator medical evaluations.

👤

Drug & alcohol testing

Pre-employment and for-cause screening, MRO-reviewed, for staff with facility requirements.
