Return to Duty
Colorado

After a DOT drug or alcohol violation, Colorado drivers must complete a SAP evaluation, a negative return-to-duty test, and a follow-up testing plan before resuming safety-sensitive duties. WorkOccMed coordinates the whole process.

Why It's Required

Getting Back on the Road in Colorado

A DOT drug or alcohol violation — including a positive test, refusal, or FMCSA Clearinghouse flag — prohibits a CDL driver from performing safety-sensitive functions until the full return-to-duty process is complete.

Colorado's CDL workforce: With freight moving along I-25, I-70 and I-76 and the I-70 mountain freight corridor and the Denver logistics hub anchoring the state's logistics economy, Colorado's construction, energy, aerospace, and distribution sector depends on drivers getting back to compliant, safety-sensitive duty quickly. Colorado’s mountain routes place added emphasis on driver fitness and DOT medical standards.

The Process

Five Steps to Return to Duty

1

SAP Evaluation

A Substance Abuse Professional evaluates the driver and recommends education or treatment.

2

Education / Treatment

The driver completes the SAP's recommended program.

3

Follow-Up SAP Evaluation

The SAP confirms compliance and clears the driver for return-to-duty testing.

4

Return-to-Duty Test

A negative, directly-observed test at a certified site near the driver in Colorado.

5

Follow-Up Testing Plan

A minimum of 6 unannounced tests over the next 12 months, as set by the SAP.

FAQ

Return to Duty Questions — Colorado

How long does the return-to-duty process take in Colorado?

It varies by driver — the SAP's recommended education or treatment sets the pace. Once cleared, the return-to-duty test itself can typically be scheduled same-day at a certified Colorado site.

Who reports the return-to-duty test to the Clearinghouse?

WorkOccMed reports return-to-duty and follow-up test results to the FMCSA Clearinghouse on your behalf as your TPA.

Does WorkOccMed provide the SAP evaluation itself?

We coordinate with SAPs and manage the paperwork, records release, and testing logistics; the SAP evaluation itself is performed by a qualified, independent Substance Abuse Professional.
