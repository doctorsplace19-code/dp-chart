Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026  –  WorkOccMed LLC

1. Introduction

WorkOccMed LLC ("WorkOccMed," "we," "us," or "our") provides occupational health scheduling and compliance services, including DOT physicals, drug and alcohol testing, and consortium management, connecting employers and individuals with a nationwide network of certified collection sites. DOT Physical and WorkOccMed clinical services are provided by WorkOccMed Medical Group, PLLC. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through workoccmed.com and our related forms and portals, how we use it, and the choices available to you — including our text messaging (SMS) program.

2. Information We Collect

When you fill out a form on our site (for example, a partner application, clinic application, corporate account request, or contact request), we collect the information you provide, which may include your name, email address, phone number, company or clinic name, and any details you enter about your request. We also collect standard technical information (such as browser type and pages visited) through normal website operation.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries and applications submitted through our forms
  • To schedule, confirm, and remind you about appointments and account requests
  • To provide customer support and answer questions
  • To operate, maintain, and improve our website and services
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

4. Text Messaging (SMS) Program

If you provide your mobile phone number through a WorkOccMed web form, you may opt in to receive text messages from WorkOccMed related to your inquiry — including appointment confirmations, scheduling reminders, account updates, and customer support follow-up.

Opt-in: By submitting your phone number on a WorkOccMed form, you consent to receive text messages from WorkOccMed at that number regarding your request.

Message frequency: Message frequency varies based on your activity with us (for example, appointment reminders are only sent if you have a scheduled appointment).

Message and data rates: Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opt-out: You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message we send. You may also contact us at the phone number or email below.

Help: Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or contact us at (888) 233-4567 or info@workoccmed.com.

Carriers: Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

No sharing of opt-in data: No mobile information, including text messaging originator opt-in data and consent, will be shared with any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes. This does not apply to information shared with our subcontractors and vendors solely for the purpose of facilitating the SMS program on our behalf (for example, our messaging platform provider).

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate our website, scheduling systems, and messaging program (such as our hosting, email, and SMS platform providers), and with certified collection sites and clinics as needed to fulfill a scheduling request you initiate. We may also disclose information if required by law.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect the information you provide to us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, and you may request that we delete information you've submitted by contacting us using the details below.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for individuals 18 years of age and older and are not directed to children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
