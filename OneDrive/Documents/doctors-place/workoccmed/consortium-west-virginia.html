Why It's Required

FMCSA Random Testing in West Virginia

Every DOT-regulated employer — including single-truck owner-operators — must participate in a random drug and alcohol testing program under 49 CFR Part 382. Operating without one risks fines that start at $16,000+ per violation.

West Virginia's trucking footprint: West Virginia freight moves along I-64, I-77, I-79 and I-81, anchored by the I-77/I-79 corridors through the Appalachian freight routes. With energy, chemicals, manufacturing, and logistics driving demand for CDL drivers statewide, West Virginia’s mountain freight routes place a premium on driver health and certification.
