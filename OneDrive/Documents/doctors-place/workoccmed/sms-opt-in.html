SMS Opt-In & Text Messaging Consent

Work OccMed LLC — how our text messaging program works, and how to opt in or out.

Our Text Messaging Program

If you provide your mobile phone number through a Work OccMed web form — such as a booking request, partner application, corporate account request, or contact request — you may opt in to receive text messages from Work OccMed at that number. Messages may include appointment confirmations and scheduling reminders, account and order updates, and occasional information about our services and consortium plans.

Opt-in method: You opt in by checking the consent checkbox below (or the equivalent checkbox on our booking and application forms) and submitting your phone number to us. Consent is not a condition of purchasing any service.

Message frequency: Message frequency varies based on your activity with us — for example, appointment reminders are only sent if you have a scheduled appointment.

Message and data rates: Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opt-out: You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message we send, or by contacting us at the phone number or email below.

Help: Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or contact us at (888) 233-4567 or info@workoccmed.com.

Carriers: Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

No sharing of opt-in data: No mobile information, including text messaging originator opt-in data and consent, will be shared with any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes. This does not apply to information shared with our subcontractors and vendors solely to facilitate the SMS program on our behalf (for example, our messaging platform provider).
