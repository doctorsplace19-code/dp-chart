Common questions

Do I need to be a DOT-regulated employer to order testing?

No. Any employer can order drug and alcohol testing through Work OccMed, regardless of whether your workforce is subject to DOT/FMCSA regulations.

How fast are results?

Most negative results are available within 24–48 hours. Non-negative results take slightly longer due to mandatory MRO review and, when needed, lab confirmation.

Can I set up a random testing pool for my company?

Yes — create a free employer account and we can help you set up a random selection schedule that fits your workplace policy.

Do you offer instant/rapid testing?

Yes, instant on-site testing is available at many of our 15,000+ locations, with lab confirmation automatically triggered for any non-negative result.
